How we calculated it

From PDF bulletins to a deployment plan in eight steps. Scroll: each step animates as you reach it.

1
REAL DATA

Collect & check the counts

For each year we took the latest weekly Johor epidemiological bulletin and copied the cumulative “Tuberculosis (all forms)” row for the 10 districts. Every year, the 10 district numbers add up exactly to the state total printed in the bulletin.

2
REAL DATA

Turn counts into rates

Cases ÷ population × 100,000. Population: DOSM (2020+) and WorldPop 1 km grids summed within district borders (2014–2017).

3
REAL DATA

Score A: steady the rates (Empirical Bayes)

Small districts can swing by chance. Each rate is pulled toward the Johor average by an amount that depends on how small the district is. Watch the orange (raw) dots move to blue (smoothed).

4
ESTIMATED

Score B: how much TB should there be?

WHO estimates Malaysia's true TB incidence (97 per 100,000 in 2023), including undiagnosed cases. We share that total across districts by population × risk. Risk uses relative risks for diabetes (1.60), undernutrition (1.96) and alcohol use disorders (3.33). District prevalence data are not yet available, so today every district has risk = 1.

5
ESTIMATED

The hidden gap

Expected rate minus reported (smoothed) rate. A big positive gap means many cases are probably not being found.

6
≈ APPROXIMATE

How far is care?

For every 1 km population cell, the straight-line distance to the nearest of 399 clinics and hospitals (OpenStreetMap), converted at 40 km/h and averaged by population. Real road journeys are longer, so read these as relative, not exact.

7
EXPLORATORY

Clusters & diagnostic deserts

Local Moran's I (queen neighbours, 999 permutations) tags each district as HH, LL, HL or LH. A diagnostic desert scores one point each for: LH (low TB beside high TB), gap above median, and travel above median. 3 points = strong, 2 = possible.

8
ESTIMATED

Where to put X-ray units

A greedy optimiser adds, one at a time, the clinic that brings the most not-yet-covered expected TB within 60 minutes (40 km). We compare it with a version that only maximises population. On the Explore page you can try your own sites.

Validation: our honest scorecard

Pass rules were fixed in the README before any TB values were extracted. We report every result.

Data sources

  • Johor State Health Department weekly epidemiological bulletins (2014–2017, 2023–2024)
  • DOSM district population (2020+); WorldPop 1 km (2014–2017, 2020)
  • WHO TB burden estimates (Malaysia, 2023)
  • Franco et al. 2024 (Cochrane): diabetes, undernutrition · Imtiaz et al. 2017: alcohol
  • OpenStreetMap health facilities · geoBoundaries district borders

Limitations

  • Bulletins for 2018–2022 missing; 2014, 2016, 2017, 2024 are part-year
  • Score B uses population share only until district risk-factor data are added
  • Travel time is straight-line at 40 km/h, not the road network
  • Clustering is exploratory with only 10 districts
  • V1, state-level V4 and V5–V10 not completed; V4 run by district (logged deviation)
