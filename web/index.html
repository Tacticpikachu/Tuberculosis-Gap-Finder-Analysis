How it works, in 4 steps

REAL DATA

1 · Count

TB notifications per district from the state's weekly bulletins, checked against printed state totals.

REAL DATA

2 · Steady the rates

Small districts' rates are gently pulled toward the Johor average so random swings don't mislead.

ESTIMATED

3 · Expect

WHO's national TB estimate is shared across districts to show how many cases each should have.

ESTIMATED

4 · Reach

Travel distance to the nearest clinic and an optimiser decide where X-ray units cover the most expected TB.
