Decision architecture

How TB Gap Finder works.

Every district goes through five engines before we suggest an action. Each step is labelled real, estimated or exploratory.

ENGINE 01
📄

Bulletin counts

TB notifications per district read from the Johor State Health Department's weekly bulletins and checked against printed state totals.

ENGINE 02
⚖️

Steady rates (Score A)

Empirical Bayes smoothing pulls small districts toward the Johor average so chance swings don't mislead the ranking.

ENGINE 03
🧮

Expected TB (Score B)

WHO's national incidence estimate shared across districts by population and risk, giving the hidden gap: expected minus reported.

ENGINE 04
🛣️

Distance to care

Population-weighted distance from every 1 km cell to the nearest of 399 clinics. Combined with the gap to find diagnostic deserts.

ENGINE 05
🚐

Unit siting

A greedy optimiser places 1–5 portable X-ray units where they cover the most expected TB within 60 minutes. Or pick your own.

CHECKS
✅

Honest validation

Forecast accuracy, better-than-chance and death-rate checks against rules fixed before analysis. Failures are reported too.
